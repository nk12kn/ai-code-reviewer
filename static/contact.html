Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We welcome responsible disclosure from security researchers. If you identify a security issue in this web service or its backend endpoints:

  • Please select "Security Vulnerability Report" in the form or email directly.
  • Include clear steps to reproduce (HTTP requests, payload, affected endpoint).
  • Allow reasonable time for investigation and patch deployment before public disclosure.
  • Do not engage in denial of service (DoS), automated rate-limit abuse, or unauthorized access to other users' data.
